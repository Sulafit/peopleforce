Server-side progress rules, an XP ledger that never pays out twice, passwordless sign-in.

5 / 10

The stack in one breath: a Next.js web app, a Python FastAPI backend on PostgreSQL, and lesson content managed in Sanity so editors can change the journey without a deploy. The AI sits behind a provider abstraction, its output is validated before it is used, and every call is traced in Langfuse. The backend, not the browser and not the LLM, decides progress and XP, and rewards are idempotent. Sign-in is a passwordless email link sent through Resend, and the quiz a visitor took before signing up is linked to their account. It all runs on Docker Compose through Dokploy, with product analytics in PostHog.
